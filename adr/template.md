# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Affects:** repositories or modules this binds

## Context

What forces the decision. The constraint, the problem, the thing that is currently
painful. Facts, not preferences - anyone reading this in two years should be able
to tell whether the context still holds.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated in the active voice. One paragraph if possible.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were genuinely on the table and why each lost. This is the part
that stops the same argument from being had again.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what work this creates. Include the
cost - a record that lists only benefits is a sales pitch, not a decision.

## Revisit when

The condition that would make this decision wrong. If you cannot name one, the
decision may be too vague to record.
